KneeAI 3D Viewer Instructions
========================================

1. Open viewer/index.html in a web browser
2. Click 'Load GLB file' and select: results/demo/knee_3d.glb
3. Click 'Load patient profile JSON' and select: results/demo/patient_profile.json
4. Use mouse to rotate/zoom the 3D model
5. Toggle structures on/off with checkboxes
6. View measurements and implant candidates in side panels
